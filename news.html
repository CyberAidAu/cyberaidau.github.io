Data & Privacy · Published 18 September 2026

Smart glasses raise new workplace privacy and security questions

Australia is considering restrictions on camera-equipped smart glasses in government workplaces because the devices can discreetly record people, conversations and sensitive information. The discussion is a timely reminder for small organisations to decide how wearable recording devices should be handled before a problem occurs.

What small organisations can do

  • Set clear rules for smart glasses and other recording devices in workplaces and customer areas.
  • Require consent before recording staff, customers or visitors.
  • Keep recording devices away from confidential meetings, computer screens and sensitive records.
  • Explain the rules to staff and review them as wearable technology changes.

Small Business Cybersecurity · Published 18 September 2026

Perth school breach highlights the risk of retaining old personal data

A reported cyberattack on a Perth private school involved personal information belonging to current and former students, with records dating back to 2015. The incident shows how keeping historical information can increase the number of people and the amount of sensitive data exposed in a breach.

What small organisations can do

  • Keep a register of the personal information your organisation holds and where it is stored.
  • Collect only what is needed and securely delete information that is no longer required.
  • Restrict access, use encryption and monitor access to sensitive records.
  • Maintain a tested data-breach response plan and know when reporting obligations may apply.

Small Business Cybersecurity · Published 17 September 2026

AI phishing is becoming harder to detect

AI-generated phishing messages can now appear polished, personalised and credible. Small businesses should no longer rely on spelling mistakes or awkward wording as their main warning signs.

What you can do

  • Verify urgent payments and bank-detail changes through a separate, trusted channel.
  • Use contact details you already know—not those supplied in the message.
  • Enable strong multifactor authentication and keep staff training current.
  • Treat unexpected requests for money or credentials as suspicious.

AI Governance · Published 17 September 2026

Engineers call for human accountability in Australia’s AI strategy

Engineers Australia says technical expertise, infrastructure and clear human accountability should be central to national AI policy. AI may support decisions, but people and organisations must remain responsible for consequential outcomes.

Why it matters

  • Businesses need governance and oversight—not just access to AI tools.
  • High-impact decisions should have a clearly accountable human owner.
  • Safe adoption depends on skilled people, cybersecurity and reliable infrastructure.

Data & Privacy · Published 17 September 2026

Australia consults on stronger privacy protections

The Australian Government is considering further reforms to the Privacy Act. The draft package proposes stronger safeguards for individuals and clearer responsibilities for organisations handling personal information.

What small organisations should review

  • Know what personal information you collect, where it is stored and why you need it.
  • Use clear, specific consent rather than broad or pre-selected permissions.
  • Delete information that is no longer required and maintain a tested data-breach plan.
  • Remember that these measures are proposed and may change before becoming law.
