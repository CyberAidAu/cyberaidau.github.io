Meet Kimberley Tan-Nobbs.

Kimberley is a final-year Bachelor of Cybersecurity student at Torrens University Australia, based in Brisbane. She created CyberAidAU as a research-stage initiative to explore how practical, understandable cybersecurity support could better serve Australian small businesses.

She is building experience across authorised vulnerability assessment, network defence, secure software, privacy, governance, data analysis and responsible AI. CyberAidAU is not yet trading; Kimberley is using the period before graduation to strengthen her capability and establish the safeguards required before working with clients.
